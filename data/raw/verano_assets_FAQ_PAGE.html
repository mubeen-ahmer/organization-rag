1. Shipping & Delivery

Q: What are the shipping charges across Pakistan?
A: We offer flat-rate shipping of PKR 250 for all nationwide orders under PKR 5,000. Orders exceeding PKR 5,000 qualify for FREE standard shipping!
Q: How long does standard delivery take?
A: Deliveries within Lahore and Faisalabad are typically fulfilled within 24–48 hours. Orders to Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and other nationwide cities take 2 to 4 business days via TCS or Leopard Courier.
Q: Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
A: Yes! Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for all nationwide orders up to a maximum cart limit of PKR 35,000 per transaction.
Q: Can I modify or cancel my online order after placement?
A: You may request order modifications or cancellations within 2 hours of order placement by contacting our WhatsApp Customer Desk at +92-300-VERANO1 (+92 300 8372661) or emailing support@veranoapparel.pk.

2. Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

Q: What is Verano Apparel's return policy?
A: We offer a hassle-free 14-day return and exchange window from the date of package delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with original price tags and purchase receipts attached.
Q: Can I exchange an item bought online at a physical retail store?
A: Yes! You can visit any of our 3 retail outlets (MM Alam Road Flagship, Packages Mall Lahore, or Kohinoor City Mall Faisalabad) along with your original online order invoice for an instant exchange.
Q: Are sale items eligible for return or exchange?
A: Items purchased during seasonal sales at discounts of 40% or higher are eligible for size exchange only (subject to stock availability), but non-refundable. Clearance items tagged at 50% or above are final sale.
Q: How long does it take to process a monetary refund?
A: Once returned items pass quality inspection at our central warehouse, online refunds are processed within 5–7 business days via Bank Transfer, Easypaisa, or JazzCash.

3. Sizing & Product Care

Q: How do I choose the correct size for Verano jeans and t-shirts?
A: Every product page features an explicit 'Size Guide' with measurements in inches for chest, waist, shoulder width, and inseam. Our Classic Crewnecks run standard fit, while Oversized Tees feature a dropped-shoulder relaxed fit.
Q: How should I care for Verano Activewear to preserve stretch and moisture-wicking?
A: Wash activewear garments inside out in cold water (max 30°C) with mild detergent. Avoid fabric softeners or bleach, and line dry in shade. Do not iron activewear directly.

4. Verano Club Loyalty & Discounts

Q: What is the Verano Club Loyalty Program?
A: Verano Club is our free loyalty program. Earn 1 reward point for every PKR 100 spent in-store or online. Accumulate 500 points for a PKR 500 discount voucher on your next purchase!
Q: How do I redeem my staff or promotional discount code online?
A: Enter your promo code or staff verification ID at the checkout screen in the 'Discount Code' field before proceeding to payment.
